Implement a complete Push Notification system for the app without modifying existing APIs or business logic.

Requirements:

General
- Integrate Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android and iOS.
- Request notification permission during onboarding or first app launch.
- Store and update the device FCM token securely.
- Support notifications in foreground, background, and when the app is closed.
- Tapping a notification should open the relevant screen.

Customer Notifications
- Booking confirmed.
- Service accepted by the garage.
- Mechanic assigned.
- Vehicle check-in completed.
- Service started.
- Service completed.
- Invoice generated.
- Payment successful.
- Service cancelled or rejected.
- Reminder 1 hour before scheduled service.
- Promotional offers and discounts.

Garage Owner Notifications
- New booking received.
- Booking cancelled.
- Customer rescheduled.
- Payment received.
- New customer review submitted.
- Daily booking summary.
- Low inventory/service reminder (if applicable).

Admin Notifications
- New garage registration awaiting approval.
- New customer registration.
- Failed payment alerts.
- High-priority support requests.
- System alerts and errors.

Notification Center
- Add a Notifications screen.
- Display unread/read status.
- Show notification title, message, date, and time.
- Allow users to mark notifications as read.
- Support deleting individual notifications and clearing all.
- Show notification count badge on the app icon and notification icon.

Settings
- Allow users to enable or disable promotional notifications.
- Keep service-related notifications enabled by default.
- Save notification preferences.

Performance
- Avoid duplicate notifications.
- Queue notifications if the device is offline.
- Ensure reliable delivery and proper error handling.

Do not modify existing APIs, authentication, booking flow, payment flow, or business logic. Keep the implementation modular and scalable for future notification types.
