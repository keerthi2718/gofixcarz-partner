Refine ONLY the Job Card UI.

The current Job Card still looks AI-generated. I want it to feel like it was designed by an experienced product designer over multiple product iterations—not generated from a prompt.

⚠️ Very Important
- Do NOT change the user flow.
- Do NOT change navigation.
- Do NOT change business logic.
- Do NOT change functionality.
- Do NOT redesign the layout completely.
- Only improve the visual quality and craftsmanship of the existing Job Card.

The goal is to make the Job Card look authentic, human-designed, and production-ready.

Design Principles:

• Design for usability first, aesthetics second.
• Avoid perfectly symmetrical layouts.
• Avoid generic template cards.
• Use natural spacing instead of equal spacing everywhere.
• Let important information stand out through hierarchy, not color.
• Create a realistic enterprise UI, not a concept UI.

Visual Refinements:

- Refine the spacing and alignment until every element feels intentionally placed.
- Improve typography hierarchy with clear distinction between primary, secondary, and supporting information.
- Use subtle shadows and soft elevation instead of heavy borders.
- Use a consistent 16px border radius.
- Keep the card clean with generous breathing space.
- Use one neutral background and white cards.
- Keep the brand red only for accents and important actions.
- Remove anything that looks overly decorative or artificial.

Information Hierarchy:

The user's eye should naturally scan:

Customer Name
Vehicle Details
Current Job Status
Service Details
Technician
Estimated Delivery
Amount

The information should feel effortless to read.

Status Chips:

Use muted colors with soft backgrounds.

Avoid saturated colors.

Status chips should feel integrated into the card instead of floating elements.

Icons:

Use a single icon family throughout.

Icons should be subtle, properly aligned, and visually balanced.

Buttons:

Keep the existing actions.

Only refine their appearance.

Buttons should feel tactile, with proper padding, radius, and subtle press feedback.

Micro-interactions:

- Gentle fade-in when cards appear.
- Smooth state transitions.
- Soft ripple on tap.
- Skeleton loading.
- Pull-to-refresh.

Avoid flashy animations.

Most Important:

Do NOT make the card look like an AI-generated dashboard.

Avoid:
- Perfect grids
- Identical spacing everywhere
- Oversized badges
- Large empty areas
- Generic card templates
- Random accent colors
- Dribbble-style concepts
- Overdesigned UI

Instead, make it feel like a mature product that has evolved over years through real user feedback.
